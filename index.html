Welcome to Crystal Springs

The best of both worlds: riverside tranquility, minutes from town.

Nestled along the picturesque banks of the River Flesk, Crystal Springs offers an idyllic retreat where natural serenity meets warm Irish hospitality.

Crystal Springs Bed and Breakfast is proudly owned and operated by the Brosnan Family, who ensure the comfort and enjoyment of every guest by providing top-quality, spacious en-suite rooms and genuine local care.

Located in a peaceful country setting just 1 km from Killarney town centre, our property is the premier launchpad for touring the world-famous Ring of Kerry, Dingle Peninsula, and Killarney National Park (Muckross House, Ross Castle, and the Gap of Dunloe). Relax to the soothing sights and sounds of the River Flesk, stroll through our private guest gardens, or enjoy salmon and trout angling directly on location.

Local Area & Attractions

Lots to See & Do in Killarney

Renowned for its majestic mountains, historic castles, and serene lakes, Killarney has endless activities for couples, families, and outdoor enthusiasts.

1.5 km away Nature & Lakes

Killarney National Park & Lakes

Over 26,000 acres of ancient oak woods, wild red deer herds, and breathtaking boat cruises across Lough Leane, Muckross Lake, and Upper Lake.

3.5 km away Heritage & History

Muckross House, Gardens & Traditional Farms

Tour the historic 19th-century Victorian mansion, wander the world-famous rhododendron gardens, and experience traditional 1930s Irish farming life.

3.0 km away 15th Century Castle

Ross Castle & Traditional Jarvey Carts

Recently restored 15th-century stronghold on the shores of Lough Leane. Take a scenic ride in a traditional horse-drawn Jaunting Car through the park.

10–14 km Scenic Sights

Gap of Dunloe, Torc Waterfall & Ladies View

Marvel at Ireland’s most scenic glacial valley, hike the trails of Torc Mountain, and witness the iconic viewpoint named after Queen Victoria's ladies-in-waiting.

Direct Base Wild Atlantic Way

Ring of Kerry & Dingle Driving Tours

Crystal Springs is situated right on the Ring of Kerry route, providing the perfect starting and ending point for unforgettable coastal day excursions.

1.0 km (15 min walk) Culture & Dining

Town Centre Pubs, Music & Artisan Shopping

Stroll into town for lively traditional Irish music sessions, cozy gastropubs, craft boutiques, woollen mills, and Saturday local farmers' markets.

How to Reach Us

Location & Directions

Situated 1 km from Killarney town centre on the Woodlawn / Ballycasheen Road, on the banks of the River Flesk.

GPS Coordinates: N+52° 3’ 10.47”  |  W-9° 29’ 15.77”
From Cork / Waterford (N22)

As you approach Killarney on the N22, turn onto the Woodlawn / Ballycasheen Road before reaching the busy town centre.

From Kenmare & National Park (N71)

Follow the scenic N71 through Killarney National Park, connect via Muckross Road, and turn onto Woodlawn Road towards the River Flesk.

From Kerry Airport (Farranfore) & Train Station

Kerry Airport is just 17 km (20 min drive) via the N22. Killarney Train & Bus Station is 1.5 km away (3 minutes by taxi).
